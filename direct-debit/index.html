Direct Debit

Recurring bank-to-bank collections through the UAE Direct Debit Marketplace (DDS). The merchant creates a contract in the Merchant Portal, the customer signs it with UAE PASS, and Geidea collects each payment on its due date and pays the merchant out through APEX.
