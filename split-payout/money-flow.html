How each payout is calculated

Calculated by APEX, not PGW. A percentage split is first converted against the gross order amount. Each amount is rounded to 2 decimals; the partner's residual absorbs the rounding.

What the partner receives

The residual: everything left of the partner's net after vendor payouts and fees.

APEX amount validation

A scheduled APEX process, once pricing is available. Any failure rejects the whole advice with a reason code. "Split ≤ net" is not enough: it passes advices that leave the partner negative once fees are taken, and rejects valid ones where commission covers the gap.

The sequence in APEX

  1. APEX calculates the partner's settlement and deducts Geidea's MDR and VAT on the MDR. The remainder is the partner's net.
  2. For a transaction with a valid split advice, APEX calculates each vendor's payout: split amount, minus commission on the vendor definition (if any), minus the remittance fee when the partner MID is set to deduct it from vendors. Rounded to 2 decimals.
  3. A remittance fee is charged for every payout: each vendor transfer and the partner's own payout. The fee on the partner's payout always comes from its residual.
  4. Commission taken from a vendor leg stays with the partner and reaches it in the residual.
  5. APEX transfers each vendor's net payout to its IBAN and settles the residual to the partner.
Not yet defined (Finance)
  • Whether vendor payouts go through a separate staging account, or APEX posts them directly from the merchant payout account.
  • Accounting of fund movements, and VAT on vendor commission and remittance fees.

What happens in each situation

paid · normal
Transaction not flagged for split

Settles to the partner through the normal process, after Geidea's MDR.

paid · split
Flagged, valid advice processed

Vendors receive their net payouts; the partner receives the residual.

held
Flagged, advice rejected

Nothing is paid to vendors; the transaction stays held. The rejection and reason show in the split payout report. The partner may send a corrected, complete advice through the Payout Advice API.

held
Flagged, no advice received

The transaction stays held. Example: a deferred split never sent, or an advice lost in transit and not in the day-end file.

released
Flagged, no valid advice within N days

Released to the partner after Geidea's MDR and listed in an exception report for Operations and Finance. N is set per partner MID, can be zero, and counts from the scheduled payout date.

parked
Advice received before the transaction

Parked as PENDING_MATCH and linked when the transaction arrives; rejected with a reason if still unmatched at day end.

held
Advice in API but missing in the day-end file

Marked as mismatched; settlement held until resolved.

exception
Transfer to a vendor fails

Existing exception process: retry up to the configured limit, then an exception record, shown in the split payout report. Geidea contacts the partner only, never the vendor.
