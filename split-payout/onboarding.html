Onboarding

How a partner gets split payout, from request to go-live. Most steps are operational (emails and documents between teams). Two steps change system set-up: APEX creates the vendors, and PGW configures the partner MID.

Illustrative Reflects product doc v1.6, sections 5 and 7.1 Read the requirement → Stories: prerequisites P1, P2 → After set-up: End-to-end view →
Terms: partner = the Geidea merchant (MID) that asks for split payout; vendor = a payee of the split. APEX gives each vendor a beneficiary ID; PGW uses that ID to check the partner's requests.

Rules that apply

  • Offered only to select large partners, case by case. Unlicensed aggregators are excluded.
  • Risk approves the partner before any onboarding. The service is never on by default.
  • Business revisits the partner agreement with Legal: dispute and chargeback liability, refunds and chargebacks deducted from the partner, recovery of any shortfall, and domestic fund transfer terms.
  • Due diligence and screening happen once per vendor, when it is onboarded, not on every payout.
  • New vendors, changes to a vendor's details or commission, and deactivation follow the same operational route.
  • Vendors have no portal access. Geidea talks to the partner only.
  • PGW holds no vendor bank details and does not check vendor status. APEX rejects a beneficiary ID that is wrong or no longer active.
