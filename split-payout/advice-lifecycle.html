Advice lifecycle

A split advice tells APEX how to pay one flagged transaction. This page follows an advice from the moment it is sent to the moment the money moves: its statuses at APEX, what PGW records, and what happens to the money each day.

Illustrative Reflects product doc v1.6 (6.3–6.5, 7.3, 7.5) and PGW stories v0.5 (SP-7 to SP-9) Read the requirement → End-to-end view → Stories: SP-7 → SP-8 → SP-9 →
Terms: partner = the merchant (MID) with split payout on; vendor = a payee of the split. Action A creates an advice at APEX, U replaces it. PGW sends A for a split sent with the order or a Payout Advice API NEW, and U for an UPDATE. APEX advice fields and statuses are drafts until the Emcrey spec (P3).
