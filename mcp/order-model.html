Order model: one MCP order, from purchase to the last refund

Follow one AED 100.00 order through PGW. Step through the HPP offer, the purchase in the customer's currency and the refunds that close the order. See which fields PGW writes at each step, how order totals stay in AED, and what the merchant and the processor see.

Terms. Order currency: what the merchant priced in (AED). Transaction currency: what the customer pays in and what the scheme clears in. Order view of a transaction: mcpBaseAmount + mcpBaseCurrency (AED). Transaction view: amount + currency (paid currency). Rates are dummy (Visa section of a file dated 20261124, version 01). New field names are the stories' proposals, except mcpBaseAmount and mcpBaseCurrency (agreed). PGW engineering can rename them.
