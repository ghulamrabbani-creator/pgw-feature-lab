Terms. Order currency: what the merchant priced in (AED). Transaction currency: what the customer pays in, and what the scheme clears in (APEX calls it cardholder currency). Settlement currency: what the merchant is paid out in; MCP does not change it. Rate: Planet's rate from AED to the transaction currency, markup included.
Decided after PRD v2 (09-Nov), now in v3.0
  • Geolocation is MaxMind GeoIP2 (a database held by PGW), not Planet. Planet offers no geolocation (13-Nov).
  • Rates come from Planet's daily rate file (template 194). PGW quotes from the Visa section, the higher of the two rates (24-Nov).
  • The converted amount is rounded up to the currency's minor unit (24-Nov). Refunds keep the PRD rule: round down, with the last-cent rule on the final refund.
  • MCP is offered strictly on location. No proposed currency (UAE, no country, currency not offered, Phase 2) means AED only and no currency selector (25-Nov).
  • The confirmation and receipt show the amount paid and the AED order amount (25-Nov).
  • The MCP toggle is in the PGW configuration inside MA, built by the PGW team. It is switched on after Operations registers the merchant with Planet for MCP (24-Nov).
