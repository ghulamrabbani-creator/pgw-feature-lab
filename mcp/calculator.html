Terms. Order currency: what the merchant priced in (AED). Transaction currency: what the customer pays in and what the scheme clears in. Minor unit: the smallest amount of a currency (GBP 0.01). 4

Minimum refund (existing rule)

S6 check 1.2

MCP adds no minimum of its own. The existing minimum refund amount (AED 0.10) applies, as for any refund. It is enough for MCP: AED 0.10 converts to at least one minor unit in every MVP currency, because each has a rate of 0.1 or more per AED.

Decided 25-Nov-2026. A refund below AED 0.10 gets the existing refund message.

2

The quote the customer pays

S4 section 2 · S5 section 3
Rule. Order amount × rate, rounded up to the currency's decimals (decided 24-Nov). The quote is fixed for the HPP session: a newer rate file does not change it.
3

Refunds in AED, sent in the transaction currency

S6 section 1 · section 2
Rule. Non-final refund: refund AED × purchase mcpRate, rounded down. Final refund (AED refunded reaches AED paid): purchase amount − all earlier refunds, in the transaction currency (last-cent rule). Never a new rate.
