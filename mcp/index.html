MCP: dynamic multi-currency pricing

A customer on Geidea HPP is offered the price in their own currency, based on where they are, and can pay in it. The merchant prices, sees and is settled in AED as today, and never sees the conversion. Rates come from Planet's daily file, markup included. Launch target Jan-2027.

One order, three currencies: the main change

The change to discuss first. Inside PGW, the existing transaction amount and currency now hold what the customer paid (GBP 21.37). Everything the merchant sees, and APEX's main fields, stay in AED. Two new fields, mcpBaseAmount and mcpBaseCurrency, carry the AED value on every purchase and refund, so no system downstream converts anything.
