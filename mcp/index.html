MCP: dynamic multi-currency pricing

A customer on Geidea HPP is offered the price in their own currency, based on where they are, and can pay in it. The merchant prices, sees and is settled in AED as today, and never sees the conversion. Rates come from Planet's daily file, markup included. Launch target Jan-2027.

One order, three currencies

order currency

What the merchant priced

AED 100.00

Seen by the merchant, the Merchant Portal and APEX main fields. MVP: AED orders only.

transaction currency

What the customer pays

GBP 21.37

Chosen on HPP. Authorised and cleared with the scheme in this currency. APEX holds it as the cardholder currency. Never shown to the merchant.

settlement currency

What the merchant is paid

SAR

Unchanged by MCP. The order amount (AED) is converted to it for payout, as today.

Worked example from PRD v2: rate AED → GBP 0.2137, markup included.
